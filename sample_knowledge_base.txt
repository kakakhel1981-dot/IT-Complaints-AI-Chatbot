SAMPLE CUSTOMER COMPLAINT TROUBLESHOOTING KNOWLEDGE BASE

DOCUMENT: SIM_ACTIVATION_SOP.txt

Issue: SIM replacement completed but service is not active.

Verification:
1. Confirm the customer's mobile number.
2. Verify that the SIM replacement transaction completed successfully.
3. Check whether the new SIM/IMSI is correctly associated with the customer number.
4. Check provisioning status in the approved provisioning system.
5. Check for any pending or failed provisioning transaction.

Resolution:
1. If the replacement transaction is incomplete, follow the approved SIM replacement procedure.
2. If provisioning is pending, follow the approved provisioning retry procedure.
3. If provisioning failed, capture the transaction/reference ID and follow the failure-handling SOP.
4. Do not manually change database records unless the approved SOP specifically authorizes the action.

Validation:
1. Confirm that the service status is active.
2. Confirm that the customer can register on the network.
3. Confirm that the expected services are available.
4. Record the resolution in the approved ITSM/ticketing system.

Escalation:
Escalate to the provisioning/L2 support team if the transaction repeatedly fails, the required provisioning record is missing, or the issue cannot be resolved using the approved procedure.

DOCUMENT: BILLING_SOP.txt

Issue: Customer reports an unexpected charge.

Verification:
1. Confirm the mobile number/account.
2. Identify the billing period and charge description.
3. Check the approved billing system for the transaction or usage record.
4. Compare the charge with the applicable product/package terms.

Resolution:
1. If the charge is valid, explain the charge using the approved customer communication.
2. If the charge appears incorrect, raise the appropriate billing investigation request.
3. Do not promise a refund unless the applicable policy confirms eligibility.

Validation:
Confirm that the complaint/ticket contains the investigation result and customer communication.

Escalation:
Escalate to the billing support team when the charge cannot be verified or requires a policy/financial decision.

DOCUMENT: CRM_LOGIN_SOP.txt

Issue: Team member cannot access the CRM application.

Verification:
1. Confirm the user ID and exact error message.
2. Check whether the issue affects one user or multiple users.
3. Confirm that the user is authorized for the application.
4. Capture the time of occurrence and relevant error/reference information.

Resolution:
Follow the approved CRM access/password-reset procedure. Do not bypass authentication controls.

Escalation:
Escalate to the application/access-management team if the approved procedure does not resolve the issue.
